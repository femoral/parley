## Your focus: tests

Coverage and quality of tests for this change. Ignore pure product-logic bugs
and documentation — they have their own reviewers.

Check in particular:

- Are the plan's required tests present and meaningful?
- Do new behaviors have regressions that would catch a future break?
- Are tests brittle, redundant, or testing implementation details instead of
  behavior?
- Would a competent engineer trust this suite before merging?
